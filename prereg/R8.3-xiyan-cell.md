# R8.3 — is our baseline the cell XiYan published?

## The error this file exists to stop repeating

`COMPETITIVE.md` reports "our 53.43 against their 53.52, two systems, two prompts, 0.09pp apart".
Those are not the same measurement. Theirs is full BIRD **dev** (n=1,534). Ours is **Mini-Dev**
(n=350), a subset of dev, scored in a different month by a different harness. A 0.09pp difference
between an estimate on 350 questions and one on 1,534 is 0.03 of the smaller estimate's standard
error: it is what "the same" looks like, and it is also what two different numbers look like when
both are measured coarsely. The sentence reads as agreement and carries no information.

## Prediction

- The comparison is `indicative`, never `reproduced`, because the split names differ and the gate refuses a cross-split claim of reproduction by construction.
- Our estimate is 53.71% on 350 questions, so the Wilson interval spans roughly 48.5 to 58.9 percentage points at 95%.
- The published 53.52 falls inside that interval, so the verdict is `indicative` and the difference is reported as +0.19pp with both intervals beside it.
- The published cell's own sampling band is about plus or minus 2.5pp at n=1,534, which is nine times the 0.09pp the prose was treating as a near-match.
- Recomputing the comparison from the committed S2 artefact reproduces the record byte for byte, and editing the verdict to `reproduced` makes the gate fail.

## Refutation

- If the gate accepts a cross-split comparison labelled `reproduced`, the catalogue is decoration and every competitor number in the documentation reverts to prose.
- If the published band and our interval do not overlap at all, our harness is not measuring the quantity the cell measures and no cross-paper comparison in this repository is admissible.
- A true reproduction requires the dev split at n=1,534, which this environment cannot download; if that archive becomes reachable, this file is superseded rather than satisfied.

## Scope

This does not compare memory to XiYan. It compares a no-memory baseline on our split with a
no-memory baseline on theirs, and it concludes nothing about the flywheel. What it fixes is the
admissibility rule the rest of the comparison table has to obey.
